===== Objective =====

Introduce Gazebo Harmonic and the ROS 2/Gazebo integration without yet introducing underwater physics or the NATS application bridge.

Reuse the simple robot from the robot-description lesson where practical. This isolates Gazebo integration from ROV complexity.

<code text>
Xacro / URDF
    ↓
ROS 2
    ↓
ros_gz
    ↓
Gazebo
</code>

===== Exercises =====

  - Start Gazebo reliably.
  - Spawn the simple robot.
  - Inspect visual and collision geometry.
  - Confirm the ROS 2/Gazebo connection.
  - Introduce simulation time and understand ''/clock'' and ''use_sim_time''.
  - Establish a repeatable launch procedure.

Do not introduce realistic hydrodynamics, NATS, Cockpit, Control, or hardware yet.

===== Exit criteria =====

  * [ ] Gazebo starts reliably.
  * [ ] The model spawns.
  * [ ] ROS 2 and Gazebo exchange the required data.
  * [ ] Simulation time is understood and works for relevant nodes.
  * [ ] The launch sequence is repeatable.
