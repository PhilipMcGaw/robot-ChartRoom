===== What the build uses =====

The [[https://dojofordrones.com/pi-zero-drone/|Drone Dojo Pi Zero drone guide]] describes a 250 mm quadcopter build. The guide identifies ArduCopter as its flight software and a Matek F405-STD as the flight controller. A Raspberry Pi Zero W is connected to the flight controller over a serial port as a companion computer. The Pi does not run the flight-control firmware in this design.

ArduPilot's [[https://ardupilot.org/plane/docs/common-matekf405.html|Matek F405-STD documentation]] currently lists a dedicated MatekF405-STD firmware target. It also warns that the F405 board's flash-memory limit means that not all ArduPilot features are included. The exact flight-controller variant matters; ArduPilot lists different firmware targets for the STD and CTR versions and does not support the older AIO and OSD variants by default.

===== Relevance and limits =====

This is a documented example of using a small Raspberry Pi as a companion computer beside an ArduPilot flight controller. ArduPilot describes companion computers as Linux-based onboard computers communicating with the autopilot over MAVLink.

The guide is for ArduCopter on a small aerial vehicle. It does not establish that this flight controller, wiring, or configuration is suitable for ArduSub or an underwater vehicle. The guide also notes that its F405-STD board was being discontinued; check current availability and exact board revision before using the build as a parts list.
