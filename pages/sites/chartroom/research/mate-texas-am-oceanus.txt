===== Scope =====

Team Oceanus is the Texas A&M underwater robotics team within ThinkTank. Its public repository describes a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) and companion vertical profiling float for the MATE ROV Competition. This page summarises the repository's own overview; it does not independently validate the team's design or implementation.

===== What the repository contains =====

The repository is organised as a team monorepo with subteam work, ROV computer-aided design (CAD), wiring schematics, technical data, documentation, float firmware, and a prior-season archive. The team describes an eight-thruster vectored vehicle with six degrees of freedom, a Navigator flight controller with Raspberry Pi 5, BlueOS and ArduSub, and piloting through QGroundControl over MAVLink.

The software overview includes a software-in-the-loop (SITL) ArduSub simulation of the vehicle for control testing before hardware. Named subteam areas include guidance, navigation and control; electrical power; thermal mechanisms and structures; communications and computing; and fluids and propulsion. The repository says the guidance/control and communications/computing areas are planned to merge, so the listed organisation may change.

===== Relevance =====

This is a useful whole-project reference because one repository brings together software, vehicle CAD, wiring, float firmware, and team documentation. The README gives a quick architecture map; the simulation and subteam directories offer paths for deeper review of control, electronics, mechanical work, and cross-team boundaries.

===== Limitations =====

The repository represents a competition project and includes a previous-season archive alongside current work. Its top-level descriptions are team-authored and may change during the season. Check the date, branch, and subteam status of a specific file before treating it as the team's current design.

===== Visual references =====

The repository's CAD and documentation directories are the team's source for vehicle visuals. The repository does not show a clear licence for reuse of its media, so use its pages to view the current material rather than copying images into Chartroom.
