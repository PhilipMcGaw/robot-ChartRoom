===== Product overview =====

The supplied product link identifies the Pimoroni Enviro Weather station kit, which uses an Enviro board with a Raspberry Pi Pico W integrated. The board contains a BME280 sensor for temperature, pressure, and humidity, and an LTR-559 sensor for light and proximity. RJ11 connectors attach the separate wind and rain sensors. Pimoroni describes the kit as including the sensors, a weatherproof cover in the form of a Stevenson screen, a battery pack, and other setup items.

The manufacturer describes wireless setup and logging, with data able to be sent to databases, home-automation dashboards, or citizen-science projects. The board includes a real-time clock (RTC) for timed wake and deep-sleep operation. Pimoroni reports 20 µA sleep current when the board is asleep; this is a manufacturer specification, not an independently measured result. The product page describes its software as pre-installed firmware and links to a getting-started guide.

===== Relevance and limits =====

Enviro Weather is a useful integrated reference for a compact, battery-powered outdoor weather station. It can help compare the Smart Buoy and weather-station work on sensor integration, low-power scheduling, wireless provisioning, and data forwarding. Its built-in sensors cover basic atmospheric measurements; wind and rainfall require the separate external sensors included in the selected kit.

The product page says the Stevenson screen needs modification to pass the sensors' RJ11 connectors through its base. The electronics are intended for installation inside a suitable weatherproof enclosure; the board itself is not described as waterproof. This is a surface-weather station reference, not an underwater sensor package.

Pimoroni marked the kit as no longer stocked on the product page when reviewed on 2026-11-08. The linked page and guide describe the Pico W Aboard product range, but do not establish current firmware maintenance, ongoing availability, or support status. Verify current documentation and stock before treating the kit as obtainable.
