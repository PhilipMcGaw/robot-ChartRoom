This section contains research notes, external references, design inspiration, and investigation records.

Research is **not** automatically an engineering decision or requirement. Treat each page as evidence or reference material unless the page explicitly records a later decision or validated result.

===== How to use this section =====

Research pages should make the following clear where relevant:

  * the source or sources;
  * what was directly observed or documented;
  * what is interpretation or engineering relevance;
  * limitations and uncertainty;
  * whether the material is merely reference, a research candidate, or has progressed into project work.

Prefer primary sources and stable official documentation. Preserve provenance rather than silently converting secondary reporting into project fact.

===== Research status =====

The status vocabulary used elsewhere in Chartroom applies here:

  * **reference** — useful external material or design inspiration;
  * **research candidate** — under consideration for further investigation;
  * **planned** — a defined project investigation has been scheduled;
  * **validated** — evidence has been obtained for the specific project context.

A research page does not establish implementation, physical manufacture, commissioning, or production validation by itself.

===== Current research =====

Research notes are maintained as individual pages so that provenance and uncertainty remain attached to the relevant subject.

Use the repository search or the navigation tree to locate a specific research topic. When a research result materially affects architecture or implementation, record the resulting decision in the appropriate authoritative document rather than leaving the decision implicit in a research note.
