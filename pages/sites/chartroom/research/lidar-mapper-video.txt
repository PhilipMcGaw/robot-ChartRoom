The user flagged this video as an intriguing example of a LiDAR Mapper. The video has not yet been reviewed in detail, so this note deliberately does not assert its hardware, software, performance, or intended operating environment.

===== Why review it? =====

Assess whether the mapper offers useful ideas for the Robots project, particularly:

  * how the LiDAR sensor is mounted, scanned, and integrated with the rest of the system;
  * mapping method, localisation requirements, and whether simultaneous localisation and mapping (SLAM) is used;
  * processing hardware, software dependencies, and data formats;
  * map quality, range, update rate, and limitations in representative environments;
  * power, size, mass, cost, and mechanical integration; and
  * possible relevance to terrestrial robots such as MarsBot or SwarmBot, and whether any aspects transfer to other survey or ROV-related work.

These are review questions, not claims about the video or the demonstrated system. Do not assume that a terrestrial LiDAR mapper is suitable for underwater operation.
