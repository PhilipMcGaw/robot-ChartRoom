===== Source summary =====

Alpha and Omega Semiconductor's application note MOS-013, *Application of Power MOSFETs in Battery Management Charge-Discharge Systems*, describes power MOSFET arrangements used with a dedicated battery-protection integrated circuit in lithium-ion battery protection modules. The note is by Quan Li and Elin Gao, revision 1.0, dated August 2021.

The note compares low-side and high-side placement, drain-to-drain and source-to-source back-to-back pairs, and N-channel and P-channel choices. It also discusses parallel MOSFETs for higher current, redundant protection arrangements, thermal design, short-circuit capability, avalanche capability, and immunity to high voltage slew rates (dV/dt). Its examples provide a useful overview of switching and protection concepts.

===== Relevance and limits =====

This is a component application note for protection circuits, with examples framed around consumer-electronics battery packs. It is not a complete battery-management-system design or a validated design for a robot, K9, or remotely operated vehicle (ROV). The examples do not establish suitable ratings, protection thresholds, wiring, fuse coordination, or fault behaviour for any project pack. Those choices require a design based on the actual cells, pack voltage, load current, charging system, and applicable safety requirements.
