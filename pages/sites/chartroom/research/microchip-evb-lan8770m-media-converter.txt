===== Candidate use =====

The user is considering the Microchip EVB-LAN8770M_MC development board (EV02N47A; RS stock number 209-7642) to connect a control PC to the ROV over 100BASE-T1 single-pair Ethernet. This is a research candidate only. It has not been purchased, integrated, or tested with the ROV or its tether.

Microchip describes the board as a media converter between 100BASE-TX on an RJ45 connector and 100BASE-T1 on a single-pair interface. It can let a conventional Ethernet host connect to a compatible 100BASE-T1 endpoint. A single EVB-LAN8770M_MC does not provide both ends of the link: the ROV must itself have a compatible 100BASE-T1 PHY and Ethernet interface, or use a suitable second media-conversion arrangement. Compatibility with the ROV computer and its network stack has not been established.

===== Manufacturer-documented setup =====

The Microchip evaluation board user's guide (document DS50002979A, 2020) states:

  * the board is powered through micro-USB;
  * the RJ45 side uses a Category 5 Ethernet cable and must link at 100 Mb/s, not 10 Mb/s;
  * the single twisted-pair 100BASE-T1 connection uses screw terminal J9;
  * the 100BASE-T1 link requires one end configured as master and the other as slave; on this board, J3 open selects master and J3 closed selects slave;
  * jumpers J6 and J11 must be fitted for normal operation when USB-powered.

These are evaluation-board setup details, not a confirmed installation procedure for the ROV. Confirm the board revision and its guide before wiring. The guide's setup uses a single twisted pair; the cited material does not establish suitability of a particular underwater tether, connector, cable length, shielding, or environmental enclosure.

===== Assessment and open checks =====

The board appears to provide a practical PC-side conversion point if the ROV side already supports 100BASE-T1. Before selecting it, identify the ROV-side MAC/PHY hardware and its master/slave configuration, then confirm the tether pair and connector meet that interface's requirements. Bench-test link establishment and sustained traffic at the intended cable length, and test recovery after disconnects and power cycles. Assess whether this evaluation board is suitable for the intended control-station installation and operating environment.

This proposal concerns the Ethernet physical link only. It does not establish network addressing, routing, control-software compatibility, bandwidth under video load, or safe behaviour on link loss. The existing [[sites:chartroom:architecture:communications|communications architecture]] page describes the planned embedded RS-485 bus; this 100BASE-T1 candidate would be a separate Ethernet path and is not an adopted replacement for that bus.
