===== Reference summary =====

The Printables listing by OFD - OldFusionDesigns describes Flycore Angled as a mostly complete foam-dart blaster core for integration into a custom design. The creator presents it as a way for designers to focus on the surrounding shell and configuration. The listing says its licence is Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike (CC BY-NC-SA); check the current terms before reuse.

===== Relevance =====

This is a design reference for modular foam-dart projects, including the broader zombie-themed activity idea recorded in [[sites:chartroom:research:printables-nerf-dart-turret-brushless-wifi|the Printables Wi-Fi turret note]]. It describes a handheld blaster core, not a robot turret or a project-approved payload. No integration, safety, performance, or competition suitability has been established by Chartroom.
