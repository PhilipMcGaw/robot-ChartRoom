===== Overview =====

ESPHome is an open-source system for building firmware for connected devices from component configurations. Its official site describes a visual Device Builder and YAML configuration workflows, with support for sensors, switches, displays, and other components. It documents installation and wireless over-the-air updates, and promotes integration with Home Assistant over the local network.

The component documentation covers a range of microcontroller families and interfaces. The available boards, components, and installation methods depend on the selected device; check the official documentation for the intended hardware before designing around a feature.

===== Relevance =====

ESPHome is a possible reference for small sensor or control nodes, including home and environmental sensing prototypes. The [[sites:chartroom:research:solar-powered-wifi-weather-station-v40|Solar Powered WiFi Weather Station V4.0]] article names Home Assistant with ESPHome as one of its ways to view or forward readings.

This is a research reference only. Chartroom does not record ESPHome as adopted or tested for CuttleOS, SquidLink, or NautiPi. Its documentation does not establish suitability for real-time vehicle control, the project's communications architecture, low-power remote deployments, or marine environments.
