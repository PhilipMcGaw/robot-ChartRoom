===== Board overview =====

Humans for Robots describes the 3DoT as a compact, Arduino-compatible controller for small robots. The manufacturer's site presents it as an integrated platform with onboard processing, motor-control and servo connections, battery charging, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), and expansion through add-on shields. The published board documentation identifies an ATmega32U4 microcontroller and describes the battery, connectors, switch, and motor/servo limits.

The documentation states that the motor outputs supply 0–5 V and are intended for 3–6 V motors with no-load current below approximately 200 mA. It warns that the onboard battery has a 2 A limit for the motor and servo supply. An external supply connection is documented for 4–18 V, with a solder bridge change required to power the motor supply externally. Check the applicable board revision's schematic and instructions before connecting hardware; the available online manual is labelled 3.1 and may not describe every later board revision.

===== Relevance and limits =====

The 3DoT is a useful reference for compact ground robots and teaching projects that benefit from integrated low-power motor control, servo outputs, wireless control, and expansion shields. It is not a substitute for a Raspberry Pi-class companion computer where Linux, ROS 2, or substantial image processing is required.

The sources describe a small robot controller and do not document a pressure-rated enclosure or underwater use. Treat it as a ground-robot electronics reference; do not assume the bare board is suitable for an ROV. The manufacturer's product description and documentation were not independently tested as part of this research.
