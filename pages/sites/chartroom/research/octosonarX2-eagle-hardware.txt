The OctoSonar project includes EAGLE schematic and board files for its OctoSonarX2 hardware. The directory also contains top- and bottom-side images and a short readme. The OctoSonarX2 is described by the project instructions as a 16-port controller for HC-SR04 ultrasonic range sensors. It is a historical design reference, not a project design requirement or validated hardware for the Robots project.

The directory readme identifies the files as the “1.0.0 batch”. It says this batch changed the bypass capacitors and rerouted power using a wider track. It also notes that the power track passes closer than ideal to the bottom-left mounting hole and that a plastic screw was supplied, with a fix expected in a later batch. The available note does not establish whether that later revision was made.

The schematic and board files have not been electrically or mechanically reviewed here. No fabrication, licence, or project-specific suitability assessment is recorded.
