===== Reference summary =====

Roger Cheng's article describes using aluminium extrusion for the main structural members and custom 3D-printed brackets where project-specific geometry is needed. Its example is a robot rocker-arm subassembly with angled connectors. The article also discusses printed-part orientation, fastener placement, and trade-offs between generic nuts and extrusion-specific fasteners.

===== Relevance and limits =====

This is a useful concept reference for robot frames and suspension prototypes that combine standard metal sections with custom connectors. The article is not a design specification or a test report for any Robots project. Printed-part strength depends on material, print process, geometry, loading, and environment; the article does not validate a particular bracket for a project vehicle.
