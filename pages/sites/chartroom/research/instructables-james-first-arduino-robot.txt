The original [[https://www.instructables.com/James-Your-first-Arduino-Robot/|Instructables: James – Your first Arduino Robot]] page exposes its title and a 12-step format, but its step text was not readable during review. The copy's visible summary lists an Arduino Uno, a prototyping shield, M3 screws and spacers, a servo, an ultrasonic sensor, and a chassis that could be made from acrylic or alternative materials such as balsa wood, plywood, foam-core, or cardboard.

===== Relevance and limits =====

This is a possible teaching reference for planning and fabricating a simple robot chassis from accessible materials, and for mounting an Arduino board and sensor. Its limited available description does not establish how the robot moves or uses its sensor. The project has not been validated for Robots project use.
