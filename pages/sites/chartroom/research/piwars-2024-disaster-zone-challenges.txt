===== Challenge categories =====

The official Pi Wars 2024 page describes the event's theme as “Disaster Zone” and says its challenges were more-or-less the same as those announced for Pi Wars 2020. It groups the challenges by how the robot may be controlled:

  * **Autonomous-only:** Lava Palava.
  * **Autonomous or remote control:** Eco-Disaster, Escape Route, Minesweeper, and The Zombie Apocalypse. The page says autonomous attempts could earn more points.
  * **Remote control:** Pi Noon – The Hindenburg Disaster and The Temple of Doom. The page says autonomous operation was allowed for fun, without extra points.

The page also lists Blogging, Technical and Artistic Merit, and a Most Disastrous Robot special award as additional scoring opportunities. It notes that rules and points could be changed or clarified after competitor questions.

===== Teams and team blogs =====

The official [Pi Wars 2024 team list](https://piwars.org/2024-disaster-zone/team-list/) groups teams by competition category and links to team blogs where one was submitted. Use it as a directory for finding team-authored project reports. The directory does not verify the technical claims in those reports; cite and review each team's own source before relying on implementation details.

===== Relevance =====

This is a historical reference for designing varied small-robot tasks: autonomous navigation, blind-maze exploration, object detection and interaction, teleoperation, obstacle traversal, and competition documentation. These categories describe the 2024 event and should not be treated as current Pi Wars rules.

For examples of PiWars robot work, see [[sites:chartroom:research:piwars-dan-ed-opencv-blob-tracking|Pi Wars 2024 — OpenCV colour blob tracking]], [[sites:chartroom:research:piwars-footleg-motor2040-pid-mecanum|Pi Wars 2024 — Motor2040 PID control and mecanum drive]], and [[sites:chartroom:research:piwars-destructorpi-wall-detecting-simulation|Destructor Pi wall-detecting simulation]]. The competition page identifies Minesweeper as a challenge, but this note does not establish that any linked robot implementation competed in or completed it.
