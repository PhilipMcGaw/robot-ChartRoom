===== Relevance =====

The post was identified as a potentially good starting point for a **base station / control station** for the Robots project.

Review it for useful ideas around the physical operator workstation, enclosure and layout, display arrangement, input devices, portability, power, and the integration of control and monitoring interfaces. These are review prompts, not claims about the implementation shown in the post.
