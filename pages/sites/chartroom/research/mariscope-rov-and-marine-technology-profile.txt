===== Company and product scope =====

Mariscope presents itself as a marine technology supplier and manufacturer of remotely operated vehicles (ROVs), towed systems, cameras, lights, and specialised underwater equipment. Its marine technology pages describe a modular product approach, customisation, and products from compact systems to work-class vehicles. The company also lists oceanography, diving equipment, services, and custom ROV development. These are manufacturer descriptions, not independent performance assessments.

The comparison page also shows accessory options such as cable reels, sonar, ultra-short baseline (USBL) tracking, altimeters, manipulators, recovery tools, sampling tools, cameras, lighting, and data logging. Availability varies by model; consult the manufacturer's compatibility table and confirm configuration directly.

===== Relevance and limits =====

The range is a reference for commercial ROV classes, payload accessories, and manufacturer-supplied operating specifications. It may help frame questions about depth, speed, topside power, vehicle mass, and tooling. It does not establish compatibility with the Robots project's control, communications, tether, power, or topside systems. The listed 230 V AC minimum power is a supplier specification and should not be treated as the complete vehicle electrical design or as evidence of how power is transmitted through a tether.

Mariscope also lists robotic arms, including products it says it represents from Reach Robotics in Chile and Argentina. That representation and geographic availability are supplier statements; they do not establish integration compatibility with any particular ROV.
