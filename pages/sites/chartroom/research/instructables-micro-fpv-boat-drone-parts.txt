===== Project summary =====

The Instructables project by ossum describes a micro airboat made from spare micro-drone parts. The introduction and parts list reproduced by JPRalves identify a whoop-sized flight controller, two brushless motors, two 2.5-inch propellers, a canopy, and a first-person-view (FPV) camera. The project uses three-dimensional printed parts for its final design and documents earlier iterations as learning examples.

===== Relevance and limits =====

This is a small surface-vehicle concept for reusing drone components and integrating an onboard camera. It may be useful for comparing compact layouts and propulsion/control reuse in a surface craft. The accessible repost contains only the introduction and component overview; the nine-step Instructables build was not readable during review. No performance, reliability, water-ingress protection, or suitability for project hardware has been independently established.
