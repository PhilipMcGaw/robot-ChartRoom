===== Scope =====

This note records the Homebuilt ROVs Forum as a community research source. The forum index describes its purpose as exploring the hobby of building an ROV. The directory includes boards for beginner questions, computer-aided design, control systems and tethers, frames, hulls and buoyancy, thrusters, and cameras.

The forum's **Control Systems and Tethers** board (`f=15`) is a large discussion area for control boards, controllers, and tethers. Search-indexed content reported 323 topics when reviewed on 2026-11-08. The supplied URL requests the board with `start=25`, but the paginated page itself returned a cache miss; no topics are attributed to that specific page here.

===== Relevance =====

The forum may help locate first-hand build discussions and practical questions across mechanical design, propulsion, control, tethering, and underwater imaging. Forum posts are individual contributions; they do not by themselves establish safe designs, verified performance, or generally applicable specifications. Assess specific claims against primary documentation and test evidence before relying on them.

===== Thread: ROV schematic and questions =====

In a 2017 [[https://www.homebuiltrovs.com/rovforum/viewtopic.php?t=1887|Control Systems and Tethers thread]], a first-time builder described a proposed tethered ROV loosely based on OpenROV. The builder wanted to send power over the tether to charge the vehicle battery during operation and shared topside and full schematics. The post proposed a 400 m tether made from a 24 AWG pair, carrying an expected 3 A, with rectified current used to trickle-charge a 5 Ah, 3-cell lithium-polymer battery. Questions included generating a safer alternating-current voltage, transferring power over the tether, power-line communications at that distance, and whether the proposed charger-regulator could manage battery power flow.

These are the poster's design goals and questions, not measured performance or a validated design. The attached schematics have not been electrically reviewed here. A reply says that 48 V is considered the maximum safe voltage underwater, but gives no supporting source; do not use that forum statement as a safety limit. Any tether-power design needs an independently sourced electrical-safety assessment and calculations for the specific cable, voltage, current, losses, isolation, charging system, and fault conditions.

===== Thread: Easiest way to control ROV over Cat5 =====

In an [[https://www.homebuiltrovs.com/rovforum/viewtopic.php?t=1888|August 2017 thread]], a builder asked about hosting an operator web page on a Raspberry Pi or Arduino, sending browser button commands to an Arduino motor controller, and carrying analogue video over a Category 5 (Cat5) tether. A reply linked to Blue Robotics and ArduSub as a more turnkey route. Another reply suggested using a camera with its own web server or opening separate pages for video and controls, so that video delay would not directly affect the control page. These are suggestions in the discussion; no implementation or latency measurements are reported.

One reply links to [[https://github.com/Moki38/Moki-ROV2|Moki-ROV2]], which is documented separately in [[sites:chartroom:research:moki-rov2-software|Moki-ROV2 software reference]]. The link establishes that the project was shared as an example in this discussion, not that its approach was assessed or recommended by the other participants.
