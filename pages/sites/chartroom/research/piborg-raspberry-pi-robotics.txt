===== Scope =====

The user supplied the [PiBorg website](https://www.piborg.org/). Direct access returned HTTP 403 through the available web tool on 2026-11-08, so the current product catalogue and availability could not be reviewed.

Raspberry Pi publications describe PiBorg as a maker of Raspberry Pi educational robots and add-on boards. A Raspberry Pi article from 2016 presents ZeroBorg as a motor-controller and sensor board for the Raspberry Pi Zero, with four independently controlled motors, I²C expansion, and analogue inputs. A later Raspberry Pi Official Magazine interview discusses PiBorg robot kits, motor controllers, add-on boards, motors, and wheels, and names MonsterBorg, DiddyBorg, and ThunderBorg. These sources establish historical product context only; they do not confirm current availability or support.

===== Relevance =====

PiBorg is a reference for Raspberry Pi-based educational rovers and motor-control add-ons. Its historical material may help when comparing rover packaging, motor-control interfaces, and beginner build ecosystems. Check the current PiBorg site, product documentation, and software repositories before selecting or reusing a specific board or kit.
