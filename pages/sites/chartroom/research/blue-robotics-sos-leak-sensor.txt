===== Product summary =====

Blue Robotics lists the SOS Leak Sensor as product SKU BR-101072, hardware revision R1. It uses four sponge-tipped probes that can be placed at likely water entry points and at the bottom of a watertight enclosure. The manufacturer lists an operating voltage of 3.3–5 V, maximum current draw of 20 mA, a three-pin 0.1 in (2.54 mm) output header, and four two-pin JST-GH probe connectors. When a leak is detected, the signal is pulled high to VCC and a red indicator LED turns on.

The product page says the output can be integrated with Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Pixhawk, and other 3.3 V or 5 V devices. This is a manufacturer compatibility statement; check the selected controller's input limits, pin assignment, cable orientation, and software configuration before connecting it.

===== ArduSub leak failsafe =====

The ArduSub website now displays an archive notice. It says the documentation is no longer maintained and directs readers to the ArduPilot wiki. Its [[https://www.ardusub.com/introduction/hardware-options/additional-peripheral-devices/leak-sensor.html|legacy leak-sensor page]] says sensors can connect through auxiliary servo pins configured as general-purpose input/output (GPIO), with a failsafe configured to warn or surface the vehicle. Treat that page as historical guidance and check current documentation and the selected firmware version before applying its setup.

The current [[https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/internal-leak-failsafe.html|ArduPilot Sub internal leak failsafe page]] describes up to three analogue or digital detectors. It identifies the input with a LEAKx_PIN parameter and configures the dry-state logic with LEAKx_LOGIC; firmware versions at or below ArduSub 4.5 also require LEAKx_TYPE to declare the input type. Digital sensors should use digital inputs, including servo-channel pins only when the flight controller supports configuring them as GPIO. The page also documents leak reports received through MAVLink SYS_STATUS messages, including for sensors in a separate enclosure.

ArduPilot lists FS_LEAK_ENABLE actions as disabled, warn only, or enter SURFACE mode. The page describes SURFACE mode as the response to a leak, but this remains a configured software action and does not guarantee that a particular vehicle will recover or reach the surface. Pin assignments, logic levels, firmware support, and vehicle behaviour need to be checked and tested for the selected hardware and software versions.

Blue Robotics' [[https://bluerobotics.com/learn/sos-leak-sensor-installation/|installation guide]] describes one specific setup using Pixhawk AUX OUT 6 and selecting “Pixhawk Aux6” in QGroundControl, with dry-state logic configured as low. This is product-specific guidance; confirm that it matches current ArduPilot parameters, flight-controller pin configuration, and firmware before use.

After a leak event, the manufacturer instructs users to dry the probes fully before reuse and notes that the sponge tips wear and need replacement. Probe placement should account for the enclosure layout, penetrators, seals, and low points where water may collect.

===== Relevance and limits =====

The SOS Leak Sensor is a useful commercial reference for enclosure leak detection, replaceable probes, and linking a physical leak input to an operator warning or vehicle response. The product and guide do not establish compatibility with every project controller, input pin, BlueOS version, or enclosure. It has not been selected or validated for a Robots project vehicle.
