===== Source and scope =====

Modell-Uboot-Spezialitäten is a German-language specialist supplier for model submarines and related electronic and mechanical components. The supplied [[http://modelluboot.de/|website homepage]] timed out during review on 2026-11-08. A 2012 product catalogue hosted on the same domain was accessible and contains several entries explicitly intended for camera robots, research submarines, ROVs, and home-built vehicles. This note records that historical catalogue, not a current product listing.

===== ROV-relevant catalogue entries =====

The [[https://modelluboot.de/Anleitungen/Katalog_ModellUbootSpezialitaeten_2012.pdf|2012 Modell-Uboot-Spezialitäten catalogue]] lists a 45 mm ducted propeller for DeepRover, DF Aviator, ROVs, and home builds. The catalogue claims 4 N of static thrust at 17 W and gives typical operating data of 12 V, 1.5 A, and 7,000 rpm. It describes the nozzle and propeller as resin parts.

The same catalogue describes a complete sealed motor pod for camera robots, research submarines, and surface vessels. It specifies a 500-size electric motor driving a ducted propeller and claims about 7 N static thrust at 12 V and 2 A. Listed dimensions are 55 mm diameter by 140 mm long, with a mass of 345 g in air and about 100 g in water. An illustrated five-pod arrangement is described as able to turn on the spot and move by tilting around the longitudinal and transverse axes.

The catalogue also documents a reversible peristaltic pump and elastic-bladder ballast system. It lists a 100–300 ml ballast volume for 2–5 kg model submarines, or as a trim cell for larger models. The description claims the stopped pump prevents gradual water transfer and lists safeguards for radio-link loss, low voltage, water ingress, and a pressure switch intended to prevent overfilling. These are catalogue descriptions, not independent assessments of the control or safety functions.

===== Ballast Tank Switch =====

The supplied [[http://modelluboot.de/Elektronik/BTS.html|Ballast Tank Switch (BTS) product page]] could not be fetched during review because the site's robots.txt was unreachable. The manufacturer's [[https://www.modelluboot.de/Anleitungen/AnleitungBTS_E.PDF|BTS user manual]], marked issue 7.2000, was available. It describes a receiver-channel decoder that switches a motor forward, off, or backwards, for uses including a ballast pump, a piston tank, or two solenoid valves. The manual says loss of the radio signal triggers the “blow” output after a selectable delay of 0, 5, 10, or 30 seconds. It also describes a low-voltage trigger and a water-contact input that inhibits diving. A separate DRServo version uses piston-position feedback for proportional ballast control.

These functions and electrical details are the manufacturer's claims in a historical manual; they have not been independently tested, and the 2000 issue does not establish current product availability or suitability for a present-day vehicle. Treat this as a historical control-system reference, not as validated failsafe or ballast design guidance.

===== DELTA research-submarine model =====

The supplied [[http://modelluboot.de/KITS/DELTA_Ordner/DELTA_E.html|DELTA model page]] was not accessible during review. The manufacturer's [[https://www.modelluboot.de/Anleitungen/Uboot_Katalog_6.pdf|submarine catalogue]] and [[https://modelluboot.de/Anleitungen/Anleitung_DELTA.pdf|DELTA assembly manual]], issue 2.2005, describe a 1:8 scale model of the DELTA research submarine, about 580 mm long and 133 mm wide. The catalogue lists an operating mass of 4.2 kg, including 1.2 kg of keel ballast. It also claims that the pressure-hull tube is dimensioned for a 9.5 m “destructive diving depth”; this is a catalogue claim, not a stated safe operating depth or independent test result.

The assembly manual says to make the bow and stern cones free-flooding because they are not sufficiently pressure-resistant. It describes an elastic-bag ballast system with a reversible pump, pressure switch, water detector, and Ballast Tank Switch (BTS) control, alongside the propulsion, control, and pressure-hull assembly. This makes the kit a useful historical example of whole-vehicle integration and of separating flooded fairings from the pressure hull. It is a model submarine, not a remotely operated vehicle (ROV).

The design details, depth figure, and protection functions are supplier documentation from 2005–2006 and have not been independently verified. They do not establish the condition or availability of a current kit, nor the pressure capability of any other hull or component.

===== Delphin model =====

The supplied [[http://modelluboot.de/KITS/Delfin_Ordner/Delfin.html|Delfin model page]] was not accessible during review. The manufacturer's [[https://modelluboot.de/Anleitungen/Katalog_ModellUbootSpezialitaeten_2012.pdf|2012 catalogue]] calls the model “Delphin” and describes a 1:10 reissue following an earlier 1:12 version. It lists a displacement of 2.54 kg, a 500-size motor, four lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO₄) cells, independently controlled diving planes that can also provide roll control, and space for a small ballast tank.

The manufacturer's [[https://www.modelluboot.de/Anleitungen/Anleitung_Delphin_4_10.pdf|assembly manual]], titled “Delfin”, describes a configuration with a propeller beneath the hull, passive flippers, a split tail fluke operated as depth and roll control, and a small rudder behind the propeller. It describes a two-part glass-fibre hull, flexible polyurethane fins, and an aluminium bayonet closure. Its suggested equipment differs from the 2012 catalogue: the manual specifies a six-cell nickel-cadmium battery, three standard servos, and two attitude-control modules, and gives 480 g as an initial ballast estimate with a target residual buoyancy of 50–100 g. These may describe different kit versions; the sources do not explain the differences.

**Relevance:** a historical example of an underwater model using thrust for forward motion and tail control surfaces for depth and roll. The manual's ballast and control details are supplier guidance, not independently verified performance or a validated design for another vehicle. The accessible source material does not establish current kit availability.

For model-submarine pressure hulls, the catalogue lists computer numerical control (CNC)-machined bayonet closure rings with O-rings and gives several diameter options. The closure is described as providing access to internal equipment while retaining a circular pressure-tube cross-section. This is a model-submarine product reference; the catalogue does not establish suitability for a particular ROV depth or pressure housing.

===== Relevance and limitations =====

The catalogue is a useful historical reference for compact ducted propulsion, motor-pod packaging, variable ballast, and serviceable pressure-tube closures. Its units and configuration details can inform comparisons and further research, but the listed thrust, mass, waterproofing, and safety claims have not been independently verified. Do not use the catalogue as a current specification or a validated design basis without current manufacturer documentation and tests for the intended configuration.
