===== Reported build =====

Raspberry Pi News profiled Niels Affourtit's home-built remotely operated vehicle (ROV) on 2016-02-04. The brief article says the vehicle used a Raspberry Pi, an HD camera, 3D-printed propellers and a camera-tilt mechanism, a Bosch BMP180 pressure sensor, and an ADXL345 three-axis accelerometer. The system was controlled over an Ethernet tether.

The article reports that a webpage displayed tilt, roll, heading, temperature, and internal pressure data, and that the vehicle had been tested to 12 m with a deeper design target. It also links to video footage. These are statements in a short Raspberry Pi News profile; the linked video was not reviewed here, and the project was not independently inspected or tested.

===== Relevance and limits =====

This is a historical example of combining a Raspberry Pi, tethered Ethernet control, live camera video, and onboard sensor telemetry in a home-built ROV. The article does not provide a build manual, wiring details, pressure-housing design, depth-test method, or enough evidence to validate the reported depth. Treat it as a project lead and architecture illustration, not a reproducible or validated ROV design.
