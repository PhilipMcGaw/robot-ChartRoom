===== Resource hub =====

The Blue Robotics homepage is a manufacturer resource hub for the BlueROV2 remotely operated vehicle (ROV), BlueBoat uncrewed surface vessel (USV), components, sensors, sonars, cables, enclosures, and support material. Its navigation links to official documentation for BlueOS, Cockpit, ArduSub, and ArduRover; product setup and integration guides; community forums; and engineering tools including pressure-to-depth, buoyancy, pressure-vessel, and voltage-drop calculators.

Use the homepage as a route to current primary documentation, then cite the specific product, manual, or guide that supports a technical claim. Product listings and site navigation can change; this note does not validate individual specifications or establish a project design choice.
