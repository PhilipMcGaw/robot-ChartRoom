# Testing

Testing strategy, test environments, and verification practices for the Robots ecosystem.

## BlueOS and Blue Robotics Cockpit simulation

The [BlueOS and Blue Robotics Cockpit SITL test](blueos-cockpit-sitl-test.md) defines a hardware-free trial of their ArduSub integration. It is a comparative research test; it does not change the NATS-based Robots architecture or establish CuttleOS compatibility.
