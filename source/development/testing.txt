===== BlueOS and Blue Robotics Cockpit simulation =====

The [[sites:chartroom:development:blueos-cockpit-sitl-test|BlueOS and Blue Robotics Cockpit SITL test]] defines a hardware-free trial of their ArduSub integration. It is a comparative research test; it does not change the NATS-based Robots architecture or establish CuttleOS compatibility.
