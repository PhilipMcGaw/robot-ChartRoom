# PiWars Robot

## Project overview

This project records a family entry into PiWars, the UK robot competition. The team consisted of Philip, Tamarisk, and their two children, B and N.

The project was designed around the competition's key requirement that the robot use a Raspberry Pi Foundation product as its main controller. The robot was developed as a family activity, combining design, fabrication, programming, testing, and competition preparation.
