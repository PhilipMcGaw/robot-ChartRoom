# K-9 timeline

Changes to the K-9 versions throughout the Doctor Who timeline. This page was migrated from the PhilipMcGaw.com K-9 timeline and is now maintained in ChartRoom as part of the prop and storyline research record.

For the structured comparison of narrative continuity and physical prop evidence, see the [K-9 prop and storyline tracker](../research/k9-prop-and-storyline-tracker.md).

## Marius and K-9 Mark I

Professor Frederick Marius created the first K-9 in the year 5000 while working on asteroid K4067. In *The Invisible Enemy*, Marius explains that he could not take his real dog into space because of weight restrictions, so he built K-9 as a companion.

Expanded prose accounts describe components including a medical computer, responsive artificial intelligence, anti-radiation cladding, probes, a laser scalpel, vision and voice systems, and scanning antennas. These expanded details are continuity claims from prose sources and are kept separate from the screen-prop evidence.

Marius offered K-9 to the Fourth Doctor because he could not take Mark I back to Earth. After travelling with the Doctor and Leela, Mark I stayed on Gallifrey with Leela at the end of *The Invasion of Time*.

### Mark I appearance

The original timeline describes Mark I as light grey-silver with a black rear control panel.

Associated screen appearances:

- *The Invisible Enemy*
- *Image of the Fendahl*
- *The Sun Makers*
- *Underworld*
- *The Invasion of Time*

The timeline also recorded appearances in the *K9* spin-off series, including *Regeneration*, *Curse of Anubis*, and *Mind Snap*. These entries require separate production and prop verification.

## K-9 Mark II

The timeline describes Mark II as the same basic prop with a darker gunmetal-grey finish. The rear control panel was reported as body-coloured and lower in the shell at some points.

The rear panel was removable for transport, so its apparent height can vary between filming sessions. This is an important prop-continuity caution: not every visual difference represents a new build or narrative redesign.

Associated appearances:

- Season 16: *The Ribos Operation*, *The Pirate Planet*, *The Stones of Blood*, *The Androids of Tara*, and *The Armageddon Factor*
- Season 17: *Destiny of the Daleks*, *The Creature from the Pit*, *Nightmare of Eden*, *The Horns of Nimon*, and *Shada*
- Season 18: *The Leisure Hive*, *Meglos*, *Full Circle*, *State of Decay*, and *Warriors' Gate*
- BBC Christmas Tape
- BBCi webcast of *Shada*

By the time Mark II left with Romana in *Warriors' Gate*, the rear controls were reported as raised.

## K-9 Mark III

The timeline places Mark III briefly with the Fourth Doctor and Adric before K-9 was given to Sarah Jane Smith.

The *K9 and Company — A Girl's Best Friend* appearance is described as the same basic prop with a slight blue repaint. The neck tubing also appears metallic blue in that production.

Associated appearances:

- *K9 and Company — A Girl's Best Friend*
- *The Five Doctors*
- *School Reunion*
- *Search Out Science*

The *Search Out Science* material appears to show a green tint, possibly caused by filming, and different ears with ribs across them and three inner loops removed.

For *School Reunion*, the timeline identifies the prop as the original dummy refitted with a new head and working parts, painted a rust-spotted grey-blue, with the ear loops restored. This is a prop-history claim requiring production-source verification.

## K-9 Mark IV

The timeline describes Mark IV as the older 1977 hero prop returning in a worn condition, retaining the newer ear design and a brighter blue finish associated with *K9 and Company*.

Associated appearances:

- *School Reunion*
- *Journey's End*
- *The Sarah Jane Adventures*: series 1, series 3, and series 4

The timeline reports that the original prop received a significant electrical and drive-system overhaul by Series 3 of *The Sarah Jane Adventures*. It also records later cosmetic changes including the identification tag and a third rear handle.

The Mark III/Mark IV boundary should be treated as a production-history classification rather than proof of a wholly new physical prop. The same hero prop may have been repaired, repainted, refitted, and reclassified over time.

## Colour and appearance summary

| Period or label | Reported appearance | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| Mark I | Light grey-silver body with black rear panel | Timeline visual claim |
| Mark II | Darker gunmetal-grey body; rear panel position varies | Timeline visual claim |
| Mark III | Slight blue tint; later ear, head, and finish changes | Timeline visual claim |
| Mark IV | Older/worn hero prop; brighter blue finish; later electrical and drive overhaul | Timeline visual and production-history claim |

Lighting, film stock, repainting, duplicate units, detachable panels, and camera angle can all affect apparent appearance.

## Display and replica evidence

The original timeline records an Andrew Beech replica photographed at the Doctor Who exhibition in Cardiff.

It also records a Mat Irvine K-9 displayed at the National Film and Sci-Fi Museum in Milton Keynes, configured to represent the later *Sarah Jane Adventures* and television appearance. The photographs show later physical details including a third rear handle.

Display and replica examples are useful for documenting surviving physical interpretations, but they must not be treated as direct evidence for the screen appearance of a particular episode.
