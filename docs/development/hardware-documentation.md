# Hardware documentation

Chartroom records the information needed to understand, deploy, operate, maintain, and troubleshoot robot hardware. It does not replace the engineering definition of the hardware held in NautiPi.

## COTS components

For commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) components, use the manufacturer's official documentation as the primary reference.

Where a manual, datasheet, installation guide, configuration guide, or application note is publicly available from the manufacturer, Chartroom should link to the manufacturer's website rather than host a copy.

A hardware documentation entry should identify, where applicable:

- manufacturer;
- manufacturer part number or model;
- document type;
- document title;
- document revision or publication date, when available; and
- official manufacturer URL.

This approach keeps links authoritative and reduces the risk of distributing obsolete or third-party copies of documents.

If the manufacturer removes an older document, an archived copy may be retained only where redistribution is permitted by the applicable licence or other rights. The archive should record the source and document revision.

## Project hardware

NautiPi remains authoritative for hardware that the project designs or modifies, including:

- schematics;
- PCB designs;
- bills of materials;
- CAD;
- manufacturing files;
- embedded hardware projects; and
- hardware revision history.

Chartroom may link to those records and add the operational information needed to use the hardware.

For example, NautiPi may contain the design of a motor-controller interface, while Chartroom may contain the manufacturer's manual, deployment notes, wiring reference, commissioning procedure, and known troubleshooting information.

## Robot-specific records

When a component is fitted to a particular robot, Chartroom may record which model and revision is installed, provided that information is useful for deployment or maintenance. The authoritative physical configuration remains in the appropriate hardware records.

## Documentation licensing

Third-party documentation remains subject to its own copyright and licence terms. The Chartroom repository licence applies to original Chartroom content, not to manufacturer's manuals, datasheets, images, or other third-party material.
